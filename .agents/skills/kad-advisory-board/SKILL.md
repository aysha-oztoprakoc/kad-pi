# `kad-advisory-board` — 5-Lens KAD Advisory Board

Stress-test high-impact decisions across five independent specialized advisory lenses.

## The Five Advisory Lenses
1. **Architecture**: Modularity, deep module interfaces, seam integrity, and acyclicity.
2. **Security & Authority**: Trust domains, mutation lease safety, credential protection, and attack surface.
3. **Economics & Routing**: Token efficiency, TOKENMAXXING, and local-first execution.
4. **Verification & Testing**: Test completeness, TDD seams, deterministic reproduction, and failure modes.
5. **Epistemic Risk & Provenance**: Knowledge authority boundaries, claim classification, and anti-poisoning.
