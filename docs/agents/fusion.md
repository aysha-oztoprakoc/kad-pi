# KAD Fusion adapter

Fusion is optional and OMP-hosted. `bin/kad-fusion setup` writes `.omp/fusion/manifest.json` with upstream commit `01a348202482cad0e7d3c34eada180f711aaddd7`. Economy uses two real `omp models` IDs; Standard and Frontier are explicit stacks. Children invoke `omp --headless --json`; ambient extensions, skills, and rules are disabled by contract.

Mutation is fail-closed unless both a Fusion writer lease and KAD/workctl mutation authorization are present. This adapter does not replace KAD authority or alter ordinary `omp`.
