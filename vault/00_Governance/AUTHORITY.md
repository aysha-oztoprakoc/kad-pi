# Authority contract

`Obsidian canonical vault > compiled KnowledgePlane > indexes/projections > agent context`.

RAW_EVIDENCE is immutable evidence, not accepted truth. Human approval is required before canonical knowledge. PROPOSAL_UNREVIEWED, rejected, stale derived output, raw text, and legacy pages are excluded from normal retrieval. DERIVED output cannot promote itself. UNKNOWN remains UNKNOWN.

Agents may read approved canonical notes and write proposals/derived outputs. They may not approve proposals or directly publish canonical knowledge. Human approval binds exact proposal bytes and SHA-256. Any edit requires a new approval. This is an accidental/cooperative poisoning control, not OS-level hostile-process isolation.
