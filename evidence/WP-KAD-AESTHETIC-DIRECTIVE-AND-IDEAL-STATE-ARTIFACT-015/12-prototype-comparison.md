## 2. Qualitative Conclusion
The accepted KAD ISA achieves high visual identity without sacrificing technical legibility. The combination of dark crimson background planes, electric cyan data text, black framing, and restrained gold accents yields an unmistakably distinct diegetic terminal aesthetic that remains strictly functional, accessible (>14:1 contrast), and free from decorative clutter or ambient distractions.
