- Human direction is intentionally multi-track; the roadmap must use primary/secondary/later/not-now sequencing rather than inventing a single product identity.

- Public/academic presentation is supported but should not justify an interface before the underlying evidence and audience contract exist.
- Next round must resolve operating mode, distinct audiences, and how simultaneous desired capabilities are staged.

## Round 2 implications

- The interaction model is intentionally mixed: human review and browser advisory remain in the control loop; OMP performs bounded automation.
- “Foundation” now means more than backend code: it includes canonical knowledge structure, provenance, privacy/safety boundaries, and contracts that can serve both humans and agents.
- A public website, operational dashboard, GitHub agent experience, and wiki are four distinguishable surfaces. They must not share one undifferentiated product contract.

| Question | Human answer | Interpretation status |

## Round 3 implications

- Parallel tracks are acceptable only if they do not create a speculative shared architecture or outrank foundation gates.
- Public publication needs a deterministic sanitization boundary plus human review; this is a safety contract, not merely a design preference.
- PON/STC work should produce measurable research evidence while remaining implementation constraints.
- Red DATA_REIN/cybernetic is selected as lineage; no palette, component library, or framework is selected.

- OpenViking and Needle remain experimental dependencies behind KAD-owned contracts.

- The roadmap must reconcile “foundation before user-facing work” with “usable daily workflow”: the first workflow can be a narrow, human-reviewed wiki/research path built on the foundation contract, not a polished dashboard or website.
- The near-term target is now materially sharper: a governed wiki projection leading into a usable knowledge assistant.

| Question | Human answer | Interpretation status |

## Round 6 implications

- A public website, dashboard, and broad GitHub redesign are deferred. The wiki projection may proceed only as the narrow knowledge capability that serves both the human and agent audiences.
- The recommendation must preserve the governing constraints not restated in this round: no authority widening, no unqualified capability promotion, no full-repository ingestion, no unproved concurrency, and no implementation before the direction is explicitly accepted.
