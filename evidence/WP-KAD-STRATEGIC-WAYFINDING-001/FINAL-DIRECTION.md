# KAD-PI Strategic Direction — Final Human-Shaped Direction

## Verdict

`PARTIALLY_DECIDED`

The near-term direction and next workpackage are explicitly chosen. Longer-horizon sequencing, detailed track budgets, and future surface decisions remain provisional and revisitable.

## Project identity

- **Primary, near term:** multi-purpose KAD-PI across a governed research platform, daily AI workstation, local agent swarm/engineering harness, and knowledge/research assistant. The human explicitly rejected collapsing these into one identity.
- **Secondary:** public portfolio/academic project.
- **Operating model:** human review remains central; ChatGPT web-browser use provides advisory, review, and steering; OMP runs mostly Luna and Gemini Flash or DeepSeek Flash-equivalent automation. Provider/model identity does not grant authority.
- **Tie-breaker:** research validity when real trade-offs occur.

## Accepted decisions

### 1. Foundation-led multi-track roadmap

- **Status:** ACCEPTED
- **Decision:** Maintain research, engineering, and interface as three parallel bounded tracks, while foundation contracts and safety boundaries precede user-facing delivery.
- **Human rationale:** All four project identities matter; research validity dominates; foundation-first remains required.
- **Technical rationale:** Prevents one blended backlog and blocks speculative surfaces from becoming an authority or status plane.
- **Alternatives:** One unified track; foundation-only; product-first.
- **Evidence:** Human rounds 1–3; current partial KnowledgePlane/model-store evidence.
- **Dependencies:** Shared evidence contracts, explicit ceilings, and no speculative shared architecture.
- **Revisit condition:** A measured bottleneck or changed daily workflow invalidates the sequencing rule.

### 2. KnowledgePlane minimum

- **Status:** ACCEPTED
- **Decision:** The next valuable KnowledgePlane capability is a governed wiki backend/projection system, culminating in a deterministic cited project-knowledge command.
- **Human rationale:** Wiki must serve human and machine research organization; next month should yield a usable wiki/research assistant.
- **Technical rationale:** A provenance-preserving projection exercises the KAD contract while retaining canonical sources and exact fallback.
- **Alternatives:** Agent memory substrate; shared context service; local specialist qualification first.
- **Evidence:** ADR 0008; roadmap; human rounds 4–5 and 7.
- **Dependencies:** Canonical artifacts, deterministic Librarian, provenance, trust-domain and acceptance-state contracts.
- **Revisit condition:** The daily workflow proves a different capability is the actual bottleneck.

### 3. Experimental integration

- **Status:** ACCEPTED with explicit risk
- **Decision:** Specify the contract first, then include both OpenViking and Needle as independently bounded experimental slices.
- **Human rationale:** Use available tools while preserving the KAD boundary.
- **Technical rationale:** Both adapters can be tested without making either authoritative; each must fail closed/degrade independently and be exercised sequentially.
- **Alternatives:** Deterministic projection only; one adapter; keep both entirely separate.
- **Evidence:** OpenViking/Needle partial evidence and ADR 0008; human round 7.
- **Dependencies:** Contract RED tests, runtime ownership, provenance, validator, safe escalation, exact fallback.
- **Revisit condition:** Scope cannot remain one bounded ticket, or either adapter requires authority/spend expansion.

### 4. Research hypotheses

- **Status:** PROVISIONAL
- **Decision:** Prioritize lifecycle reliability/graceful degradation, notification selectivity/token efficiency, and local-first routing/premium escalation as the research portfolio.
- **Human rationale:** PON/STC are both architectural constraints and testable hypotheses.
- **Technical rationale:** These hypotheses align with existing evidence and can be measured without autonomous policy mutation.
- **Alternatives:** Synthetic-world graph performance first; defer comparative measurement.
- **Evidence:** Human round 5; PRIME_DIRECTIVE; existing routing/swarm/evidence artifacts.
- **Dependencies:** Accepted traces, deterministic baselines, telemetry, and validators.
- **Revisit condition:** First contract slice shows a narrower hypothesis is necessary for validity.

### 5. Self-improvement boundary

- **Status:** ACCEPTED
- **Decision:** Limit learning to accepted-trace evaluation and deterministic candidate generation. No autonomous authority or policy mutation.
- **Human rationale:** Preserve human control and scientific validity.
- **Technical rationale:** Model proposals remain non-authoritative; deterministic policy and human approval govern adoption.
- **Alternatives:** Human-approved prompt/routing adaptation; distillation experiments; policy/architecture evolution.
- **Evidence:** Human round 4 and explicit non-goal round 6; PRIME_DIRECTIVE and ADR 0008.
- **Dependencies:** Accepted evidence, deterministic validation, reviewable candidate artifacts.
- **Revisit condition:** A separately approved research WP defines stronger controls and evidence.

### 6. Interface and publication boundary

- **Status:** ACCEPTED
- **Decision:** Dashboard-first build and public website redesign are deferred. Future public material is sanitized, reviewed, source-linked, and excludes private/sensitive operational details. Later visual lineage is red DATA_REIN/cybernetic.
- **Human rationale:** Public sharing is personal-first, potentially for friends/family/professors, and must be as safe as possible; dashboard is human-facing; GitHub/docs are agent-facing.
- **Technical rationale:** Separate audience contracts and privacy boundary prevent a presentation surface from becoming an accidental authority or disclosure channel.
- **Alternatives:** Open reproducibility by default; multi-surface build now; public architecture only.
- **Evidence:** Human rounds 2–3 and 6; existing authority/evidence policy.
- **Dependencies:** Provenance, sanitization, human review, stable operational contracts.
- **Revisit condition:** Foundation contracts and a concrete audience need justify a bounded surface WP.

## Temporary non-goals

- No autonomous authority or policy mutation.
- No new paid APIs or PAYG escalation.
- No new framework or distributed architecture.
- No dashboard-first build.
- No public website redesign yet.
- No implementation of the recommended WP in this discussion package.
- Governing constraints remain: no authority widening, no unqualified promotion, no full-repository ingestion, no unsupported concurrency, and no model download merely for breadth.
