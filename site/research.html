Research / Epistemic Discipline

Keep the experiment visible.

KAD-PI is an experimental harness for governed intelligence. It records what was observed, what was designed, and what remains an unverified hypothesis without flattening those categories into an ungrounded narrative.

01 / Methodology

Four Research Tenets

TENET 01

Primary Source Grounding

Claims labeled SOURCE_FACT must be directly traceable to primary scientific literature with explicit DOI / arXiv citations and verbatim passage anchors.

TENET 02

Epistemic Disentanglement

Syntheses must cleanly separate primary findings from KAD architectural interpretations and speculative engineering extrapolations.

TENET 03

Zero Fabricated Citations

No synthetic papers, hallucinated bibliographic metadata, or unverified benchmark scores are permitted in the canonical research plane.

TENET 04

Counterfactual Evaluation

Longitudinal evaluation compares model actions against deterministic baseline policies without executing unauthorized paid spend or claiming unexecuted savings.
