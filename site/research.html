Research ledger

What each label means

OBSERVED

Measured behavior from an implementation or reproducible run. Receipts identify the command, environment, and result.

HYPOTHESIS

An expected benefit or explanation that still needs an experiment. It cannot silently become an architectural requirement.

EXPERIMENTAL

A tested mechanism with bounded conclusions. A green experiment does not grant authority to its adapter or model.

UNKNOWN

No deterministic evidence supports a stronger claim. Unknown is a useful state, not a presentation defect.
