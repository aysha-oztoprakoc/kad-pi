KnowledgePlane / governed projection

Useful context without a new authority layer.

The KnowledgePlane gives KAD-PI a stable place to recover exact facts, preserve provenance, and compile bounded context. Projections are rebuildable. Canonical sources remain in charge.

Sources of truth

Projections

Generated wiki records carry source hashes, epistemic classes, acceptance state, and trust metadata. Losing them does not lose the source.
