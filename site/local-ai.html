Local AI / bounded capability

Local models are tools, not sovereigns.

KAD-PI treats a model binding as a capability with identity, scope, resource constraints, and qualification evidence. A model can be present without being qualified or active.

QUALIFICATION

State beats implication.

FILE_ONLY means a declaration exists. LOADABLE means registry resources can load. QUALIFIED means evidence passed its gate. ACTIVE means runtime observation supports that claim.

FAILURE

Unavailable is visible.

A missing endpoint or degraded model reduces the affected capability. It does not silently substitute another model, widen permissions, or alter authority.

Why local?

Control the boundary.

  • Keep sensitive work inside the narrowest valid trust domain.
  • Make resource cost and lifecycle observable.
  • Preserve deterministic fallback when cognition is unavailable.
  • Record provenance instead of treating generated text as accepted knowledge.
