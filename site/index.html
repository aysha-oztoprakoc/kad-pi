01 / definition

A research platform, not an oracle.

Local-first

Local models and tools are preferred when they fit the task. Their availability and qualification remain observable states, not assumptions.

Evidence-gated

Claims are tied to durable sources, tests, receipts, and explicit epistemic classes. A model saying “pass” is not evidence of pass.

Deterministic authority

Probabilistic interpretation stays outside canonical mutation. Validation, resolution, and acceptance are explicit seams.

03 / doctrines

Four ideas keep the system honest.

PON — notify, don't poll

Relevant state changes should produce bounded notifications. Causal dependencies stay explicit and selective.

STC — declare, don't reach

Components declare coeffects and capabilities. Effects are scoped, attributable, and recoverable inside their boundary.

TDD — test, don't claim

RED → minimum GREEN → REFACTOR → VERIFY. Observable behavior outranks implementation confidence.

Graceful degradation

Failure reduces a capability and preserves the safest useful path. It never widens trust, authority, permissions, or spend.
